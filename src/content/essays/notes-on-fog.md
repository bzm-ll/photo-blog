body_zh: |
  ## 为什么喜欢雾
  
  雾把世界分成三层：前景是清晰的，中景开始变灰，远景直接消失。这种层次在晴朗的天气里几乎不可能自然出现，而在雾里，它是免费的。
  
  ## 三个实用的经验
  
  1. **曝光要偏右**。雾是浅色的，相机的测光会想把雾拍成中灰，结果整张照片发闷。加 0.7 到 1 档曝光，让雾接近白，但不要死白。
  2. **一定要有前景**。纯雾的照片没有信息量，加一块石头、一根栏杆、一个背影，画面立刻就成立了。
  3. **等风**。雾在动，等它把远处的塔露出来一半的那一秒，比连拍一百张有用。
  
  ## 后期
  
  黑白是雾最好的归宿。把高光拉起来，阴影压下去，中间的灰阶保留住——不要用曲线做太强的 S 形，雾的美感就在那一大片细腻的灰里。
body_en: |
  ## Why fog
  
  Fog splits the world into three planes: a sharp foreground, a middle distance going grey, and a background that simply disappears. On a clear day that layering is almost impossible to find naturally. In fog it is free.
  
  ## Three practical notes
  
  1. **Expose to the right.** Fog is bright, and the meter wants to make it middle grey, which leaves the frame muddy. Add 0.7 to 1 stop so the fog reads near white — but not clipped.
  2. **Always keep a foreground.** A photograph of pure fog carries no information. A rock, a railing, a figure standing in it and the frame suddenly works.
  3. **Wait for the wind.** Fog moves. The second it half-reveals a tower in the distance is worth more than a hundred frames fired blind.
  
  ## Post
  
  Black and white is where fog belongs. Lift the highlights, deepen the shadows, keep the mid-greys intact — avoid a heavy S-curve; the beauty is in that wide, quiet band of grey.
