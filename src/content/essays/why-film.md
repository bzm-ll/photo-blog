body_zh: |
  我不打算论证胶片比数码好，这个争论已经二十年没有结果了。
  
  我用胶片，主要是因为它的限制刚好是我需要的限制：一卷三十六张，冲扫要等一周，成本让我在按快门之前多想三秒。
  
  那三秒里，我会问自己：这个画面值得吗？光线再等十分钟会不会更好？我是不是只是在重复上一张？
  
  数码相机不会问我这些。它太好了，好到我可以什么都不想。
body_en: |
  I am not going to argue that film is better than digital. That argument has been running for twenty years without a result.
  
  I use film because its limits happen to be the limits I need: thirty-six frames, a week before the scans arrive, and a cost that makes me pause for three seconds before every shutter release.
  
  In those three seconds I ask: is this worth it? Would ten more minutes of light be better? Am I just repeating the last frame?
  
  A digital camera never asks me any of that. It is too good — good enough that I can stop thinking.
